    \vspace{-0.15cm}
    \justifying
    Evaluaci\'on t\'ecnica y econ\'omica para alojar el Backend API y PostgreSQL:

    \vspace{0.10cm}
    \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
        \begin{column}{0.65\textwidth}
            \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
            \tiny
            \begin{tabular}{@{} >{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash}p{0.21\textwidth} >{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash}p{0.21\textwidth} >{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash}p{0.21\textwidth} @{}}
                \toprule
                \textbf{Criterio} & \textbf{AWS EC2 On-Demand} & \textbf{Amazon Lightsail (Recomendado)} \\
                \midrule
                \textbf{Instancia Base} & \texttt{t3.small} (2 vCPU, 2 GB): \$15.20/mes & VPS Bundle (2 vCPU, 2 GB): \textbf{\$10.00/mes} \\
                \textbf{Disco SSD} & EBS gp3 (30 GB): \$2.40/mes & 60 GB SSD NVMe incluido \\
